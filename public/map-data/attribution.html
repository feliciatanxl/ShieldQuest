This is a geographic game reconstruction, not a live navigation service. Road widths, façades, void decks, park-tree positions and traffic behaviour are approximations. SHIELD mission interiors and resident narratives are fictional. Geometry reflects the supplied OSM snapshot, which may omit recent construction and temporary transport diversions.
