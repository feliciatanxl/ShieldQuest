# Code of Conduct

ShieldQuest is a game about looking out for each other, and working on it should feel the same way. This Code of Conduct applies to everyone who takes part in the project: the team, contributors, reviewers, co-design participants and anyone in our issues, pull requests, workshops or pilot sessions.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in ShieldQuest a respectful, welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

Many people involved are young, including students, youth co-designers and pilot participants. We hold ourselves to the standard you would expect in a youth programme.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the young people this game is for.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Insulting, demeaning or discriminatory comments, jokes or imagery.
- Sexualised language or imagery. Any content involving minors will be reported to the relevant authorities.
- Sharing anyone's personal information without permission, including real names, schools, photos, phone numbers or NRIC numbers of participants.
- **Pressuring anyone to disclose personal experiences** of being scammed, bullied or involved in an offence.
- **Victim-blaming.** Scams and peer pressure are the fault of those who exploit others, not the people they target.
- Posting real scam links, real phone numbers or working scam scripts.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a school or youth setting.

## Safeguarding

If anything you see suggests a young person may be at risk, tell the team privately and promptly. If someone is in danger right now in Singapore, call **999** (or SMS **70999** if it is not safe to talk). For emotional support, the **National mindline (1771)** and **Samaritans of Singapore (1767)** are available 24/7.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the repository owner [@feliciatanxl](https://github.com/feliciatanxl) on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Team SecurePi may remove content, or restrict or end anyone's participation in the project, for behaviour that breaks this Code.
