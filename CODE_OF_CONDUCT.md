# Code of Conduct

**ShieldQuest** is committed to fostering an inclusive, welcoming, and safe environment for all contributors, collaborators, educators, and youth participants.

---

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in ShieldQuest a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

Because ShieldQuest engages with educational content for young people, we maintain high standards of ethical conduct, mutual respect, and youth safeguarding.

---

## Expected Behaviour

- **Kindness and Respect:** Communicate constructively, focusing on ideas, code, and pedagogical effectiveness rather than individuals.
- **Youth Wellbeing:** Ensure that all scenario themes, discussions, and interactions foster emotional safety, empowerment, and resilience.
- **Appropriate Boundaries:** Maintain professional, transparent boundaries in all interactions involving students or youth testers.
- **Empathy and Inclusion:** Respect diverse learning paces, backgrounds, and perspectives.
- **Privacy by Default:** Respect participant confidentiality; never expose or solicit personal information.

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- **Harassment and Intimidation:** Any offensive, derogatory, or discriminatory language, comments, or personal attacks.
- **Victim-Blaming:** Scams, theft, and peer exploitation are the fault of perpetrators. Mocking or shaming individuals for being targeted or deceived is strictly prohibited.
- **Coercing Personal Disclosures:** No participant or contributor should ever be pressured to disclose personal experiences with crime, bullying, or scams. Discussions must remain grounded in fictional examples and transferable skills.
- **Doxxing or Sharing Identifiers:** Publishing or sharing private personal data (such as real names, school affiliations, contact numbers, or photos) without explicit written permission.
- **Malicious Content:** Committing or distributing real phishing URLs, functional exploit scripts, or operational tutorials for committing offences.
- **Inappropriate Adult–Youth Communication:** Private messaging, off-platform solicitations, or unsupervised communication with minor participants.

---

## Safeguarding Guidance

If an interaction suggests an individual is in distress or at immediate risk of harm, prioritize safety and report the situation to the project maintainers or appropriate institutional contacts.

Official Singapore support helplines:
- **Emergency Police:** `999` (or SMS `70999`)
- **ScamShield Helpline:** `1799`
- **Police Hotline:** `1800-255-0000`
- **National Mindline (Youth & Mental Health Support):** `1771`
- **Samaritans of Singapore (SOS 24/7):** `1767`
- **Tinkle Friend (Children Helpline):** `1800-274-4788`

---

## Reporting and Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported confidentially to the maintainers at [@feliciatanxl](https://github.com/feliciatanxl). All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled fairly and discreetly.

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove or edit comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and to suspend or terminate collaboration with individuals who engage in harmful or disruptive behaviour.
